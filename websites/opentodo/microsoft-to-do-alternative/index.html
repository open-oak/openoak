Export file

The free Microsoft To Do Export tool saves all your lists to one file, with notes, steps and dates.

How to make the file
  1. Download the tool for Windows, macOS or Linux.
  2. In Graph Explorer, sign in, choose "my To Do task lists", consent under "Modify permissions" and copy the access token into token.txt.
  3. Run mstodoexport export --completed --type json --output mstodo_export.json --token-file token.txt
  4. Upload mstodo_export.json in OpenTodo.

The tool is made by an independent developer. Your token stays on your computer. OpenTodo never asks for it.

Importing again is safe: tasks you already have are skipped.

Questions

When does Microsoft To Do shut down?

On 30 June 2027 the To Do apps stop on the web, Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android, for personal and work accounts.

What happens to my Microsoft To Do tasks?

They stay in To Do until the shutdown, but they do not move to Planner. Personal accounts cannot use Planner at all. Move them to another app before 30 June 2027.

Is there a free Microsoft To Do alternative?

Yes. OpenTodo is free and open source, and it uses the same views: My Day, Important, Planned and Tasks.

Can I use Microsoft's own export?

Microsoft has not released it yet. When it does, OpenTodo will add support for it. Until then, use copy and paste or the export tool.

Will my reminders and repeating tasks keep working?

Their details are saved in the task notes. Reminder alerts and automatic repeats are work in progress in OpenTodo.
